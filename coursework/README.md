## Detailed Project Descriptions

### 1. Factors Influencing the Adoption and Effectiveness of Drone-Based Medical Transport: A Systematic Review
* **File:** [`Medical-Drone-Transport-SLR.pdf`](Medical-Drone-Transport-SLR.pdf)
* **Context:** 60-Credit Applied Research Dissertation, Master in Management (January 2026)
* **Supervisor:** Dr Martyn Bradley
* **Scope & Method:**
  - Formulated a systematic review protocol following PRISMA 2020 guidelines to evaluate barriers to routine drone integration in healthcare logistics.
  - Screened 186 initial records across Business Source Complete, Scopus, Emerald Insight, Web of Science, and PubMed, selecting 37 core empirical studies and 9 theoretical works.
  - Assessed study quality using the Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool (MMAT 2018).
* **Key Findings:**
  - *The Sociotechnical Divergence:* High managerial interest during subsidized pilots often collapses upon transition to routine operations due to unaddressed workflow friction and regulatory constraints (BVLOS).
  - *The Validity Gradient:* Clinical evidence is robust for resilient payloads (whole blood) but degrades sharply for fragile biologicals (monoclonal antibodies, organs), where flight vibration and altitude fluctuations induce protein aggregation.
  - *Simulation Optimism vs. Ecological Reality:* Operational models systematically overestimate fleet reliability by assuming deterministic weather, clear airspace, and zero dispatch friction.

### 2. Hybronn: Strategic Planning and Simulation
* **File:** [`Hybronn-Strategic-Simulation.pdf`](Hybronn-Strategic-Simulation.pdf)
* **Context:** ULMJ719 Strategy Planning and Implementation (Group 2, World 1)
* **Scope & Method:**
  - Executive review of a 4-year strategic lifecycle for *Hybronn*, a simulated European automotive manufacturer navigating the transition from internal combustion engines to hybrid and battery electric vehicles (BEVs).
  - Applied Porter's Five Forces and PESTLE to assess European regulatory targets (Euro 7, zero-emission mandates) and supply chain pressures.
  - Implemented a differentiation focus strategy using the Resource-Based View (VRIN), investing in automation, retrofit kit grants, and targeted workforce training.
* **Outcomes Analyzed:**
  - Doubled labor productivity (42.9 to 87.5 cars/worker) through automation and above-average wage incentives (Herzberg / Becker human capital models).
  - Managed dynamic pricing trade-offs across four model lines (Volare, Forvia, Veyra, Tialon) to maintain gross margins between 25–34% and repay £600m in capital debt.

### 3. Hybronn: Organizational Dynamics Review
* **File:** [`Hybronn-Organizational-Dynamics.pdf`](Hybronn-Organizational-Dynamics.pdf)
* **Context:** ULMJ719 Strategy Planning and Implementation
* **Scope & Method:**
  - Structured academic reflection on group performance, decision-making biases, and interpersonal conflict during the simulation project.
  - Evaluated team role allocation using Belbin’s 9 Team Roles and tracked group progress through Tuckman’s development stages (Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing, Adjourning).
  - Contrasted rational decision-making against Herbert Simon’s bounded rationality and analyzed conflict resolution using Fisher & Ury’s principled negotiation framework.
  - Evaluated the group process through the Input-Process-Output (IPO) model, identifying communication breakdowns (Shannon-Weaver noise, media richness theory) and structural recommendations based on Kolb's experiential learning cycle.

### 4. Marketing Proposal: The Bears of Blueberry Forest (EQ for All)
* **File:** [`Digital-Marketing-Proposal.pdf`](Digital-Marketing-Proposal.pdf)
* **Context:** Marketing Management
* **Scope & Method:**
  - Developed a comprehensive multi-channel digital marketing campaign for an educational social enterprise promoting emotional intelligence and preventative mental health resources for children (ages 2–12) and caregivers.
  - Conducted SWOT analysis and competitor benchmarking against established mindfulness platforms.
  - Designed platform-specific distribution strategies (YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook) grounded in the peripheral route of Petty & Cacioppo’s Elaboration Likelihood Model (ELM).
  - Constructed audience personas (child "Leo" and parent "Emma"), engagement funnels, content roadmaps, and measurable KPI benchmarks (CTR, watch time, ROAS).
